## Draft grant

Subject to the conditions below, UR NOT MAD ENOUGH grants `[OUTLET]` a non-exclusive, non-transferable and non-sublicensable permission to publish the identified article once in the approved format, language, territory and publication window. All rights not expressly granted remain with the relevant rights holder.

The outlet agrees to:

1. Preserve the approved text, level of certainty, byline, source links and methodology/status language.
2. Use the approved headline and make no substantive edit, translation or graphic adaptation without written approval.
3. Credit `[AUTHOR] / UR NOT MAD ENOUGH` prominently and link visibly to `[CANONICAL URL]`.
4. Add a canonical tag pointing to the original URL for a full web republication when its platform supports one.
5. Exclude all third-party material listed above unless the outlet independently secures the necessary rights.
6. Reflect any correction or material update promptly after notification and preserve the original correction note.
7. Make no implication that UR NOT MAD ENOUGH endorses the outlet, an advertiser, sponsor, campaign or other third party.
8. Not sell an advertisement or sponsorship specifically against the article or place sponsor language in a way that appears to fund or endorse the investigation.
9. Not place the article in an automated syndication feed, resale database, AI-training corpus or other unapproved product.
10. Not sublicense, archive for a new commercial use or republish the article again without new written permission.

Ordinary editorial pages may contain general advertising if it is clearly separated and is not sold against the investigation. Either party may link to the other’s public page and accurately describe the republication.
