### A currentness trap to test explicitly

The [government mining and licensing explainer](https://yukon.ca/en/victoria-gold-updates/mining-and-licensing) remains reachable and contains older language and a December 26, 2024 worker-program deadline. It is not evidence that this deadline is open now. This illustrates why a successful page fetch is insufficient: check the event date, any later records and the controlling document. Do not reuse that deadline as an action recommendation.

## Feasibility still to establish

1. Obtain the exact records behind the four watch questions and record page/paragraph/table locators.
2. Verify Waterline access and correct project/file identifiers. Do not require a paid database or automated scraper for the pilot.
3. Back-test eight complete weeks without treating a newly retrieved old document as new publication.
4. Record time for authentication, numerical reconciliation, contradictory accounts and editorial review, not just writing.
5. Establish whether the scope can yield useful recurring updates within the proposed time budget.
6. Identify lawful, realistic reader-acquisition routes and test the exact $5 offer. No prospects were contacted in this study.
