# Outreach templates

These are starting structures, not mail-merge copy. Rewrite the opening for the recipient and keep a reporter pitch under 200 words. Use plain text, one canonical link and a small number of primary-source links.

## Shared fields

- `[FIRST NAME]`
- `[PRIOR WORK OR MANDATE]`
- `[DOCUMENTED FINDING]`
- `[IMPORTANT LIMIT]`
- `[WHY NOW / LOCAL CONSEQUENCE]`
- `[INVESTIGATION URL]`
- `[PRIMARY SOURCE URL]`
- `[SPECIFIC OFFER OR ASK]`

Use an accurate signature:

```text
[NAME]
[ROLE], UR NOT MAD ENOUGH
https://urnotmadenough.ca/
editor@urnotmadenough.ca | [ORGANIZATIONAL MAILING ADDRESS, IF REQUIRED]
Reply “no pitches” if you do not want future story notes from us.
```

Do not claim an interview, exclusive, embargo, source relationship or unpublished evidence unless it has actually been approved and secured.

## Direct reporter

**Subject:** `[PLACE/BEAT]: [DOCUMENTED FINDING]`

```text
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Your [DATE/ARTICLE OR BEAT] on [SPECIFIC SUBJECT] is why I’m sending this to you.

We reviewed [RECORD SET]. The records show [DOCUMENTED FINDING]. An important limit: [IMPORTANT LIMIT]. [ONE-SENTENCE TIMELY OR LOCAL CONSEQUENCE].

The investigation and source register are here: [INVESTIGATION URL]
Controlling record: [PRIMARY SOURCE URL]

[SPECIFIC OFFER: Would this be useful for a follow-up? I can send a compact document map / walk through the method / provide an approved excerpt.]

Best,
[SIGNATURE]
```

## Newsroom or assignment desk

**Subject:** `For [DESK/REGION]: [DOCUMENTED FINDING]`

```text
Hello,

Could you route this to the reporter or editor covering [BEAT/PLACE]?

UR NOT MAD ENOUGH reviewed [RECORD SET]. The records show [DOCUMENTED FINDING]. They do not establish [IMPORTANT LIMIT]. The finding is current because [WHY NOW].

Investigation, methods and source links: [INVESTIGATION URL]

I can provide an approved excerpt or a short source map if useful.

Best,
[SIGNATURE]
```

## Specialist or civic organization

**Subject:** `For your [TOPIC] work: documented [ISSUE] in [PLACE]`

```text
Hello [NAME/TEAM],

I’m sharing a public investigation because your work on [EXACT MANDATE] overlaps the records.

[DOCUMENTED FINDING]. The key limitation is [IMPORTANT LIMIT]. The dossier links the controlling documents and separates confirmed facts, supported inference and unresolved questions: [INVESTIGATION URL]

No endorsement is implied or expected. If the material is useful to your public-education work, you are welcome to link to it or ask about approved excerpt/republication terms.

Best,
[SIGNATURE]
```

## Affected community or Nation

**Subject:** `Courtesy copy: public records concerning [ISSUE/PLACE]`

```text
Hello [NAME/TEAM],

This is a courtesy copy because [COMMUNITY/NATION] is directly connected to the public record discussed here: [INVESTIGATION URL]

The records establish [DOCUMENTED FINDING]. They do not establish [IMPORTANT LIMIT]. We are not asking for endorsement or amplification.

If we have misstated a fact, missed a current public record or used language that obscures the community’s role, please reply or use [CORRECTIONS URL]. We will assess any correction promptly.

Respectfully,
[SIGNATURE]
```

## Named institution or oversight body

**Subject:** `Published record and corrections route — [INVESTIGATION TITLE]`

```text
Hello [OFFICE],

For awareness, UR NOT MAD ENOUGH has published an investigation drawing on [OFFICE/RECORD]: [INVESTIGATION URL]

Its central documented finding is [DOCUMENTED FINDING]. The article also states [IMPORTANT LIMIT OR INSTITUTION’S POSITION]. This is a courtesy and corrections notice, not a request for endorsement.

If a fact is wrong or a newer public record changes the status, please send the record or correction to [CORRECTIONS EMAIL/URL].

Regards,
[SIGNATURE]
```

## Republication or translation offer

**Subject:** `Republication option for [AUDIENCE]: [INVESTIGATION TITLE]`

```text
Hi [NAME],

Your coverage of [PLACE/TOPIC] makes this investigation potentially useful to your audience: [INVESTIGATION URL]

[DOCUMENTED FINDING]. Important limit: [IMPORTANT LIMIT].

We can authorize an excerpt, full republication or [LANGUAGE] translation case by case. Approved use keeps the byline, source links, correction notes and a canonical link to the original; third-party material is excluded unless separately cleared.

Would you like the 300-word excerpt or full terms? There is no obligation to use it.

Best,
[SIGNATURE]
```

## One follow-up

Use the existing thread. Prefer version A only when something useful has changed.

**A — new value**

```text
Hi [FIRST NAME] — one useful update to the note below: [NEW RECORD / LOCAL CONNECTION / STATUS CHANGE]. The source is [URL]. If it fits your beat, I can send the two-minute document map.
```

**B — no new value**

```text
Hi [FIRST NAME] — one final follow-up in case the documented [TOPIC] finding below fits your coverage. No reply is needed; I won’t continue the thread if it isn’t useful.
```

```text
L’enquête et le registre des sources : [URL DE L’ENQUÊTE]
Document principal : [URL DE LA SOURCE]

Est-ce que cela pourrait être utile pour un suivi? Je peux transmettre une courte carte documentaire ou un extrait autorisé.

Cordialement,
[SIGNATURE]
```

## French-language courtesy note

**Objet :** `Copie à titre informatif — documents publics concernant [SUJET/LIEU]`

```text
Bonjour [NOM/ÉQUIPE],

Nous vous transmettons cette enquête à titre informatif puisque [LIEN PRÉCIS AVEC LE MANDAT OU LA COLLECTIVITÉ] : [URL DE L’ENQUÊTE]

Les documents établissent [CONSTAT]. Ils ne permettent pas d’établir [LIMITE IMPORTANTE]. Nous ne sollicitons ni appui ni promotion.

Si un fait est inexact ou si un document public plus récent change la situation, veuillez répondre à ce message ou utiliser [URL/ADRESSE DES CORRECTIONS]. Nous examinerons rapidement toute demande de correction.

Respectueusement,
[SIGNATURE]
```

Have a fluent French editor review investigation-specific wording before sending. Official titles and Quebec legal terminology should match the controlling French record.

## Avoid

- “Bombshell,” “shocking,” “corrupt” or another conclusion the evidence does not make.
- “Thought you’d love this,” generic praise or a paragraph about the publication before the finding.
- Several links before the recipient knows why the story matters.
- PDFs or large image attachments in a first message.
- CC’ing unrelated reporters, officials, communities or advocacy groups together.
- Repeated nudges, false urgency or “just bumping this” without new value.
- Asking an oversight body or affected community to validate the reporting by sharing it.
