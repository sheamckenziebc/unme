_This is fictional demonstration content. It does not describe a real board, district or event._

The minutes record six decisions affecting land use, but appeared online 217 days after the meeting. The delay is documented. Whether it violated a rule is a different question.

The fictional board’s policy requires approved minutes to be public, but sets no deadline. The board says a staff vacancy and a document migration created the backlog. Nothing in the available record contradicts that explanation.

## Why the distinction matters

A long delay can still frustrate meaningful public scrutiny even when no formal deadline has been broken. Reporting should identify both facts: what happened, and what the rules actually require.
